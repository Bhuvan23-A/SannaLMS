# SannaLMS — Customer Demo Script
**Goal:** Show a potential customer the complete learning-management flow — who does what, how it all connects, and why SannaLMS beats other LMS platforms.
**Style:** Simple language. No technical jargon. Anyone in the room should understand.
**Total time:** 20–25 minutes (5 roles × ~4 minutes each, plus a 2-minute opener and closer).

---

## Demo Setup (before the customer arrives — 5 minutes)

- **Open two tabs:**
  - Student portal: `https://sannalms.sannainnovations.com`
  - Admin dashboard: `https://admin.sannalms.sannainnovations.com`
- **Login accounts** (all passwords are `Test@1234`):

| Role | Username | What they can do |
|---|---|---|
| Super Admin | `test_superadmin` | Runs the whole platform |
| College Admin | `test_collegeadmin` | Runs one college |
| Primary Trainer | `test_trainer` | Teaches and creates content |
| Teaching Assistant | `test_assistant` | Helps the trainer |
| Student | `test_student` | Learns and takes tests |

- **Before starting:** press **Ctrl + Shift + R** (hard refresh) on both tabs so you're showing the latest version.
- **Golden rule of the demo:** show one full student journey end-to-end (login → learn → test → submit → see score). A complete story beats jumping between menus.

## Part 1 — Super Admin Walkthrough (~4 minutes)

> "The Super Admin is you — the person who owns the platform. This is the 'God view' of everything."

**1. Login**
- Go to the student portal URL, log in as `test_superadmin`.
- **Say:** "Watch what happens — because of your role, you're taken straight into the admin dashboard. No confusing screens in between."
- You land on `admin.sannalms.sannainnovations.com` with the Super Admin menu on the left.

**2. The College Directory (one platform, many colleges)**
- Click **Colleges**.
- **Say:** "This is the heart of the product. One login, one platform, but **every college is completely separate**. Their data cannot mix. Watch — let me add a new college."
- Click **+ Add College**, fill in a name (e.g. "ABC Engineering College"), and Save.
- **Say:** "That college is now live with its own space, its own data, and its own admins. That's how you can onboard dozens of colleges without any extra hardware or software."

**3. College structure (Departments → Branches → Semesters)**
- Click through **Departments**, **Branches**, **Semesters**.
- **Say:** "Each college can be organized exactly the way your institution is structured — departments, branches, semesters. This is the skeleton that courses and students hang on."

**4. Oversight**
- Open **Analytics** and **Gradebook**.
- **Say:** "From here you can see how every college is performing — attendance, test scores, activity — all in one place. You're not flying blind. If a college needs help, you see it in the numbers."

**5. Platform-wide tools**
- Open **Calendar**, **Notifications**, **Chat**.
- **Say:** "The Super Admin can also schedule institution-wide events, broadcast announcements, and talk to anyone through built-in group chat. It's a full communication platform, not just a test-taking tool."

**6. The user profile menu (top-right avatar)**
- **Say:** "And your profile menu shows who you are, your role, and a clean Logout button — standard on every screen for every role."

## Part 2 — College Admin Walkthrough (~4 minutes)

**5. Certificates & analytics**
- Click **Certificates** and **Analytics**.
- **Say:** "When students finish, certificates can be generated automatically — each with its own verification code. And analytics shows how the college is actually doing."

**6. Day-to-day**
- Open **Calendar**, **Notifications**, **Forums**, **Chat**.
- **Say:** "Scheduling, announcements, forums, chat — the College Admin runs their whole campus from here. No separate tools, no email chains, no WhatsApp groups."

## Part 3 — Primary Trainer Walkthrough (~4 minutes)

> "The Trainer is the teacher. This is the person who creates the learning content and evaluates students."

**1. Login**
- Log out, log in as `test_trainer`.
- **Say:** "The trainer lands in a dashboard built around teaching — with quick links to build courses, schedule live classes, and manage gradebooks."

**2. Build a course**
- Click **Manage Courses** (opens the admin dashboard's course builder).
- **Say:** "A trainer builds a course with modules and lessons — text, videos, notes. This becomes exactly what the student sees in their portal."

**3. Create questions**
- Open **Question Bank**, click **+ Add Question**.
- **Say:** "Let's add a multiple-choice question with the correct answer marked. This question will be reused in quizzes — build once, use many times."

**4. Build a quiz**
- Open **Quizzes**, click **+ Create Quiz**, pick a title, attach questions, set a duration.
- **Say:** "The trainer assembles a quiz from the question bank and publishes it. The moment it's published, it appears in every student's portal."

**5. Create an assignment**
- Open **Assignments**, create one.
- **Say:** "Assignments work the same way. Students upload their work, and it's checked automatically for copying — it compares every submission against every other submission."

**6. Gradebook & recalculate**
- Open **Gradebook**.
- **Say:** "And here's the payoff — the trainer watches results flow in live. Quiz scores are auto-graded the second a student submits. One click recalculates everything."

## Part 5 — Student Walkthrough (~6 minutes — the star of the show)

> "Now the most important part — the student's day, from start to finish. This is where the whole system comes together."

**1. Login**
- Log out, log in as `test_student` on the portal.
- **Say:** "This is the student's home. Clean, simple, everything they need on the left."

**2. Overview & courses**
- Open **Overview**, then **My Courses**.
- **Say:** "Courses, progress bars, lessons with videos. Students always know where they stand."

**3. Code Sandbox (live wow moment)**
- Open **Code Sandbox**.
- **Say:** "Here's something most LMS platforms can't do. Students write real code — Python, C++, or Java — right in the browser, and it runs in a safe, isolated sandbox. Let's run this."
- Click **Execute Code**.
- **Say:** "Real code, real output, real-time. And it's completely safe — the code runs in a locked-down box, so students can't harm the system. Perfect for programming colleges."

**4. Adaptive Exam (the quiz the trainer just published)**
- Open **Adaptive Exam**, click **Start Quiz** on the visible quiz.
- **Say:** "This is the quiz the trainer published earlier — see, it appeared here automatically. Watch the question, pick an answer, move on. There's a timer, and it auto-submits when time runs out."
- Answer a couple of questions, click **Submit Quiz**.
- **Say:** "Submitted. The score is calculated instantly — and it's recorded in the trainer's gradebook. The quiz now shows 'Completed' so the student can track what they've done."

**5. Assignments**
- Open **Assignments**, pick the assignment, submit with a file name.
- **Say:** "The student uploads their assignment. It's checked for copying automatically — this one came back flagged, which the trainer can see and act on."

**6. AI Tutor (another wow moment)**
- Open **AI Tutor**, ask something like "What does this lesson teach?"
- **Say:** "Every student gets a 24/7 AI tutor that answers questions based on their actual course material. Doubt at 11 PM? No problem — the tutor is awake."

**7. Leaderboard & XP**
- Open **Leaderboard**.
- **Say:** "Learning is gamified. Students earn XP for taking quizzes and checking in, level up, and compete on the leaderboard. It keeps engagement high — students don't just sit through classes, they participate."

**8. Certificates**
- Open **Certificates**.
- **Say:** "Completed courses produce certificates with verification codes, so employers and anyone else can verify them instantly."

**9. Attendance**
- Open **Attendance**, pick a session, click **Check In via QR**.
- **Say:** "And attendance takes seconds — a QR check-in or GPS check-in from the student's phone. No calling roll, no paper."

## Part 7 — Why SannaLMS is Better (closing talking points)

Use these to answer "what makes you different?":

1. **One platform, many colleges.** Most LMS products handle one college. SannaLMS gives you a control room for your whole group of institutions — while keeping each college completely private. That's rare.
2. **Real learning tools, not just videos.** A built-in code sandbox, auto-graded quizzes, plagiarism checking, an AI tutor, and certificates with verification — competitors often sell these as expensive add-ons.
3. **Attendance that takes seconds.** QR and GPS check-in from a phone replaces roll calls and proxy attendance.
4. **Engagement by design.** XP points, levels, leaderboards, and instant feedback keep students actively participating — not just watching.
5. **Live, connected data.** When a trainer publishes a quiz, the student sees it instantly. When a student submits, the gradebook updates instantly. No files, no spreadsheets, no delays.
6. **Simple for everyone.** Teachers and students don't need training videos to use it. The interface is clean, and every role only sees what they need.
7. **Secure by default.** Enterprise-grade security, role-based access, and regular backups — without any extra cost.

---

## Demo Tips

- **If something takes longer than 2 seconds to load:** keep talking. "The system is fetching live data from the server..." — never show an awkward silence.
- **Let the customer drive** one step — e.g., let them add a question or start the quiz. Engagement beats perfection.
- **End with the student story.** The trainer → quiz → student → gradebook loop is the one demo people remember.
- **Have the URLs written on paper** for them, with the demo logins, so they can try it themselves later.
